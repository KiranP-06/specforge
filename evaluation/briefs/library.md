# Smart Library Portal
Members must be able to sign up and log in using their university ID.
Members can search books by title, author or ISBN.
Members can reserve a book that is currently issued to someone else.
Librarians can issue and return books by scanning a barcode.
The system shall calculate late fines automatically at 5 rupees per day.
Members should be able to renew a book up to two times.
Members can view their borrowing history.
Librarians can add, update and remove book records.
The system shall email members 2 days before a due date.
Admins can generate a monthly report of the most borrowed books.
The portal should be easy to use for first-year students.
Search results must be returned within 1 second for 95% of queries.
The system must support 1000 concurrent users during exam weeks.
